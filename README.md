## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Survival Log**. This trainer provides optional assistance with Survival Log's scavenging, inventory management, safehouse development, survival needs, time pressure, exploration, and zombie defense systems. It is designed to make the game's preparation phase and post-outbreak stronghold management more accessible without changing the core gameplay structure.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to focus on Survival Log's story, exploration, safehouse customization, survivor management, and disaster preparation with less resource pressure or combat difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Supplies** — Prevents selected survival resources from decreasing while scavenging, crafting, cooking, and managing the safehouse.
- **Unlimited Money** — Provides an unlimited spending balance for purchasing supplies, furniture, appliances, construction materials, and other available goods.
- **Freeze Preparation Timer** — Pauses the pre-outbreak countdown so you can plan routes, gather supplies, and improve the safehouse without the normal time pressure.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Prevents stamina from running out during movement, scavenging, and other stamina-based activities.
- **No Hunger** — Stops hunger from increasing while managing the safehouse and exploring the city.
- **Maximum Morale** — Keeps morale at a high level to reduce the pressure of maintaining the survivor's mental condition.
- **Unlimited Safehouse Power** — Maintains available electricity while using diesel generators, manual generators, solar power, appliances, and other safehouse systems.
- **Instant Construction and Upgrades** — Completes safehouse installations, room expansions, furniture placement, and upgrades without the usual delay or resource pressure.
- **Unlimited Crops and Cooking** — Supports farming and meal preparation by preventing selected crop, ingredient, and cooking resources from being consumed.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Inventory Capacity** — Removes or greatly increases carrying limits during supply runs, allowing more food, water, fuel, materials, and rare resources to be collected.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Player Invincibility** — Prevents damage from zombies, environmental hazards, and other dangers encountered during exploration and defense.
- **Unlimited Traps and Defense Resources** — Keeps selected defensive items available while preparing the safehouse and holding the line against zombie hordes.

### 🌍 World

- **Easy Scavenging** — Reduces the risk of supply runs by protecting the player from common exploration penalties and preserving collected loot.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the game and activating the desired options before beginning a scavenging run. Unlimited stamina is useful for extended movement and exploration, while invincibility can reduce the danger of reaching distant locations and returning with supplies.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate unlimited inventory capacity before collecting items from supermarkets, hardware stores, farmers' markets, schools, offices, and city ruins. Unlimited supplies can help preserve food, water, fuel, construction materials, crops, ingredients, and other resources used by the safehouse.

### World & Environment

The game begins with a ten-hour preparation window before the outbreak. Use the preparation timer option to plan purchases and scavenging routes, then use money and construction options to reinforce the safehouse. During the post-outbreak phase, the trainer can assist with farming, cooking, electricity, survivor support, and exploration under rain, freezing cold, blackouts, and other hazards.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate invincibility before defending the safehouse or entering areas with hostile threats. Unlimited traps and defensive resources can help maintain barricades and prepare for zombie hordes. Disable combat assistance when you want to experience the game's intended risk and resource-management balance.
